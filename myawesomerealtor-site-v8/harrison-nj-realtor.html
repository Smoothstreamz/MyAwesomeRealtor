Real estate in Harrison, New Jersey

Harrison is a compact Hudson County market next to Kearny and Newark, with the PATH station playing a major role in how many buyers and renters compare locations. The housing mix includes established homes and multi-family properties alongside newer apartments and condominium-style development, so value can vary substantially by property type, age, parking, amenities, and exact location.

Buying in Harrison

For Harrison buyers, the right comparison is rarely just price per square foot. Think about total monthly cost, property taxes, parking, condo or association fees when applicable, proximity to transit, building age, unit configuration, and whether you are buying for your own use or with rental income in mind. A newer condo and an older two-family can require completely different due diligence.

Selling a home in Harrison

Harrison sellers benefit from using comparable sales that match the property as closely as possible. A newer unit near PATH should not automatically be priced from an older house several blocks away, and a two-family should be evaluated with its unit layout, condition, parking, rental setup, and likely buyer pool in mind. The goal is to position the property against what buyers can actually choose from today.

Harrison rentals & investment properties

Harrison's transit access makes the rental side of the market important. Landlords should pay attention to current market rent, competing inventory, parking, utilities, building rules, move-in timing, and a consistent application and leasing process. Investors should also review the existing tenancy and income picture before making assumptions about future cash flow.

What makes Harrison a local market

Harrison is a good example of why local real estate cannot be treated as one generic North Jersey market. Transit-oriented development, established residential blocks, newer buildings, and proximity to Newark and Kearny create several different buyer and renter profiles within a small area.

Harrison real estate FAQ

Does Harrison have PATH service?

Yes. Harrison is a PATH station on the Newark–World Trade Center route. Buyers and renters should still compare the exact walk, parking situation, and their own commute before choosing a property.

Do you help with Harrison condos and multi-family homes?

Yes. I can help with single-family homes, condos, multi-family properties, rentals, and investment-oriented purchases. The due diligence is different for each property type.

How should I price a home in Harrison?

Start with recent comparable sales that match the property type, location, condition, parking, unit configuration, and amenities as closely as possible. Then compare that with current competition and buyer demand.
