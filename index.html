Do you work with first-time home buyers?

Yes. I help first-time buyers understand financing readiness, the search process, offers, inspections, appraisal, and closing so the process feels manageable from the beginning.

Yes. We can talk through timing, estimated value, preparation, likely sale strategy, and possible next steps before you commit to listing.
