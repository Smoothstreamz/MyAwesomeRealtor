Meet Steven Lartiga

Broker Associate with Rosa Agency Realtors, serving buyers, sellers, landlords, renters, and investors throughout North Jersey.

Real estate since 2009

I started in real estate as a young college professional after earning a bachelor’s degree in Business Administration from William Paterson University. Helping my first clients reach the closing table made it clear that this was the kind of work I wanted to keep doing.

Since then, I have helped clients buy, sell, rent, and navigate residential real estate throughout North Jersey. I later earned my broker’s license and currently work as a Broker Associate with Rosa Agency Realtors.

I especially enjoy helping first-time buyers understand the process, but I also work with repeat buyers and sellers, landlords, renters, multi-family purchasers, and clients exploring investment opportunities.

What you can expect

  • Straightforward explanations.
  • Responsive communication.
  • Local market guidance.
  • A no-pressure approach when you are still deciding.
